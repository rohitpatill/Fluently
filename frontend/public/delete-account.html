Delete your Fluently account

Applies to the Fluently app and website (fluently.fun) · Last updated 25 July 2026

You can delete your Fluently account and all of your data yourself, at any time, directly in the app. You do not need to contact us.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open Fluently and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon in the navigation).
  3. Scroll to the Data management section.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Type delete my account to confirm.

You are signed out immediately. Signing in with Google again creates a completely new, empty account that starts from onboarding.

What is deleted

Everything. The deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no soft-delete, no grace period and no archived copy:

  • Your account record — your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile picture
  • Your stored AI provider API key and model preference
  • All conversations and messages (text and voice transcripts)
  • All companion personas you created or added
  • All memory notes Fluently kept about you
  • All vocabulary, proficiency scores and score history

No data is retained after deletion, and none of it can be recovered — by you or by us. Our database and hosting providers keep their own automated infrastructure backups and short-lived server logs as a standard platform function; those are outside our direct control and are overwritten on the providers’ own schedules.

This cannot be undone. If you only want to clear part of your data, the same Data management section also lets you delete just your conversations, just your memory notes, or reset everything while keeping your vocabulary.

Prefer us to do it?

If you can’t access the app, email hello@fluently.fun from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and delete your account and all associated data, normally within 30 days.

Note on your AI provider key

Deleting your account removes the copy of your Gemini API key stored with us. It does not revoke the key at Google — if you no longer want it to exist, delete it in your own Google AI Studio / Google Cloud console.
